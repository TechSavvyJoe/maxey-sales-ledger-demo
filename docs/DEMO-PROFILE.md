# Synthetic demo profile

Research checked September 3, 2026. This document records the chosen demonstration scenario and the limits of its industry references. It is not a Bob Maxey Ford of Howell performance report or a Michigan benchmark.

## User-selected assumptions

- Approximately **$2,300 front gross** and **$1,200 commissionable F&I gross per delivered sale**, averaged across completed historical entries. The denominator is actual delivered sales, including entered $0 amounts, not financed-only sales or split-credit units.
- Vary deal amounts and monthly results around those averages. They are not minimums, guarantees, product prices, or amounts to assign to every sale.
- Winter months generally contain **12–18 fictional deliveries**; busier summer months contain **18–25**. These volumes shape fake data only. They are not a real salesperson's pace or a replacement for saved delivery/earnings goals.
- The fictional payment mix is approximately **70% Finance / 20% Cash / 10% Outside Finance**, with stable month-to-month variation. This is an industry-informed scenario, not a published three-way used-retail distribution. Through September 3, 2026, the generated 450 deliveries split 313 / 92 / 45 (69.56% / 20.44% / 10%).
- Product examples approximate 45% service contracts and 10% Tire & Wheel across deliveries, with GAP on about 43% of Finance sales. These demonstration choices are not personal performance targets or eligibility rules.
- Do not add or infer finance reserve. The selected F&I amount is commissionable gross for the demonstration; payment method alone does not establish reserve income.
- Awaiting F&I gross remains missing, not an entered $0. Incomplete recent months need not match the completed-history average.
- Research does not authorize changing the user's pay plan, bonus rules, goals, or the calculation engine.

## What the public sources do—and do not—establish

No current public **Detroit-area or Michigan used-only front/F&I gross-per-delivery average** was verified in this research. [NADA's 2025 public report](https://www.nada.org/media/4695/download?inline=) provides Michigan dealership revenue/counts, not that per-unit profit benchmark. [NADA 20 Group Live](https://marketing.nada.org/acton/fs/blocks/showLandingPage/a/4712/p/p-007a/t/page/fm/0) describes participant access to local-market profit comparisons. National or company-wide figures must not be relabeled as Michigan averages.

Thus $2,300 front/$1,200 F&I are chosen demo averages—not values established as normal local results. Accounting scope, vehicle mix, reconditioning and commissionable-gross definitions can differ.

[StoneEagle's H1 2026 report, released August 31](https://www.se-fi.com/post/stoneeagle-first-half-highs-for-f-i-pvr-f-i-income-per-dealer), reports $1,989 F&I PVR, 1.55 products per deal, 45% service-contract penetration and 10% Tire & Wheel penetration in its broad retail sample. Its [August 18 analysis](https://www.se-fi.com/post/inside-the-first-half-of-2026-s-f-i-growth-engine) reports Q2 dealership-financing share of 75.8%. These are not Michigan or used-only figures. Industry PPD covers more product categories than this app's three tracked flags; do not force the three-product measure to match 1.55.

[JM&A's Q2 2026 report](https://www.jmagroup.com/resources/operations/automotive-trends-report) identifies June F&I income as 61.8% product and 38.2% reserve within its participating-dealer sample. This demonstrates why broader F&I PVR is not automatically comparable to product-only commissionable gross; do not apply that ratio to this demo or another provider's average.

For reporting, keep all-delivery PVR separate from financed-cohort averages. Keep GAP's dealer-financed denominator explicit: [MenuMetric's definitions](https://support.menumetric.com/other) use finance deals for GAP and total units for other products. The published StoneEagle GAP percentage has insufficient public denominator detail to be treated as a like-for-like target here.
